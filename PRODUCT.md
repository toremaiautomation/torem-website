## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (frontend), n8n Cloud (automation engine), Supabase (database), Claude API via n8n (AI), Vercel (hosting and CI/CD deployment). All in one `src/App.jsx` — no routing library, page state managed in React.

## Users

Small business owners across any industry (retail, services, healthcare, hospitality) running 1–5 person crews. Primary situation: owner-operators who are the salesperson, scheduler, and service provider simultaneously — they miss inquiries after hours, on weekends, or when on a job. No IT staff. No tolerance for long setup or complex dashboards.

## Product Purpose

Torem AI is a done-for-you AI automation service that captures customer inquiries, books appointments, and provides visibility into what's working — without requiring any technical setup from the client. The business owner provides a website URL; Torem handles everything else and goes live in 3–7 days. Success means: no inquiry goes unanswered, calendar stays full, and the owner gets time back.

## Positioning

Not a chatbot widget the client configures themselves — a full automation stack (AI chat, knowledge base, in-chat booking, automated follow-up sequences, review generation, and analytics) delivered as a custom-built, done-for-you service. A competitor selling a self-serve SaaS tool cannot truthfully claim the same: Torem absorbs all setup complexity, builds each deployment from the client's own website content, and bundles the full funnel from first inquiry to booked appointment to post-service review.

## Operating Context

- Clients operate service-based businesses where phone calls and website inquiries are the primary lead channel
- Inquiries spike outside business hours (evenings, weekends) — that is when the automation earns its keep
- Booking integrates with Google or Outlook calendar; clients do not adopt new tools
- Torem handles all configuration — clients never touch n8n, Supabase, or any integration settings
- Sales motion: inbound via website, then a free 30-minute call to scope the engagement
- Based in Houston, TX; currently serving businesses nationally

## Product Principles

1. **Outcomes over features.** Clients care that inquiries convert and bookings land in their calendar — not which tools are running underneath.
2. **Done-for-you is the product.** Complexity belongs to Torem, not the client. Every step that would require a client decision or technical action is Torem's responsibility.
3. **Start with the foundation, layer in automation.** Most clients begin with AI Chat, then add booking and follow-up as they see results. Upsell follows proof.
4. **Custom-built, no templates.** Every deployment is built from the client's own website content and workflows — that specificity is a differentiator and a promise.
5. **Speed to value.** Live in days. If it takes weeks, it's a failure of process, not the client's fault.
